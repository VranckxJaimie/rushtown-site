The short version

RushTown is free to play and needs an internet connection. Play fair, be kind in chat, and pick a name that is your own. Coins, rush tokens and items are part of the game and have no money value. If something goes wrong, report it in the game or email us; you can always ask us to look at a decision again.

Your account

  • The first time you play online, the game creates a guest account tied to your phone. If you link your Google Play Games profile, you can keep your company when you change phones. If you don't, losing or resetting your phone means losing your company.
  • Writing in chat needs a linked Google Play Games profile.
  • One company per phone is played at a time. Opening the game on a second phone takes over the session.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the game (Settings → Account → Delete account) or by email.

The game and your items

  • Coins, rush tokens, speed-ups, boosters and every other item are part of the game. They have no money value, can't be exchanged for money, and can't be sold, bought or traded outside the game.
  • We keep improving RushTown, so things change: prices, timers, rewards and features. While the game is in testing, progress can sometimes be reset; the game tells you when that happens.
  • The game needs an internet connection. We do our best to keep the server running, but it can be down for maintenance or problems; the game shows a notice when it is.
  • RushTown shows optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. They are never needed to progress.
  • RushTown has no purchases yet. If we add them, they will be paid through Google Play under its terms.

Fair play

Don't cheat. That means no modified versions of the game, bots, scripts or tools that play for you, no tricks with your phone's clock, and no using bugs to get coins, tokens, items or ranks. Please report bugs you find instead.

The server checks saves. If something doesn't add up, we can remove you from rankings and events, take back what was gained unfairly, reset your progress or, in serious or repeated cases, close your account.

Company and alliance names

Company and alliance names may not use brand names or other people's names, or pretend to be RushTown staff. We can change such a name without notice. Names must also follow the chat rules below. If we change your name, you get a free rename.

Chat rules

You accept these rules before your first chat message. They apply to alliance chat, private chats, group chats and chat requests.

  • Be kind. No insults, hate, threats or sexual talk.
  • Keep it in the game. No phone numbers, emails, links or invites to other apps.
  • Keep it private. Don't share anyone's real name, address or school, not even your own.
  • No spam or ads, no brand names, and don't pretend to be someone else or RushTown staff.
  • Private chats, group chats and chat requests follow the same rules. You can decline, block or report anyone.
  • See something wrong? Report it. We read every report.
  • Breaking the rules can mute your chat, close it, or close your account.

Private chats are only possible with friends (both added) and members of your own alliance. Someone you haven't chatted with before has to send a chat request first, which you can accept, decline or block. You can turn private messages off in the game's settings.

Reporting and moderation

  • How to report. Use the report button on a message, a player or an alliance in the game. You can also report anything you think is illegal, or that breaks these terms, by emailing support@rushtowngame.com. Please say what it is, where you saw it (the player, alliance or message, and when), why you think it breaks the rules or the law, and your name and email. We handle reports quickly, carefully and without bias.
  • How we moderate. A word filter blocks contact details, links and abusive words before messages are sent. A message that several players report is hidden automatically until someone reviews it. Reviews and decisions are made by a person.
  • What we can do. Remove a message, mute chat for 24 hours or 7 days, close chat for an account, reset a name (with a free rename), remove progress gained unfairly, or close an account.
  • Telling you why. When we act on your account or content, the game tells you what we did, which rule it broke, whether a report, our filter or an automatic step was involved, and how to appeal.
  • Appeals. If you think we got it wrong, email us within 6 months with your player ID (in the game under Settings → Account), and a person will look at it again. You can also use an out-of-court dispute settlement body certified under the EU Digital Services Act, or go to court.
