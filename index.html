The game

From the first farm to a whole valley

Start with one village and a single van. Grow a company that keeps villages, towns and a harbour city supplied, one delivery at a time.

01 / 04

Production chains

Grain to flour to bread, sheep to wool to clothes. Every good has a chain, and every chain needs its places. Mills by the river even work faster.

02 / 04

Your own fleet

Vans and trucks drive real roads between farms, workshops, warehouses and towns. You plan the routes, stop by stop.

03 / 04

Homes move up

Households move in when you keep them supplied. Bring the right goods and building materials, and homes climb their ladder all the way to tower villas.

04 / 04

A valley to open up

New villages and towns come out from under the clouds as your homes move up, from the hills down to the sea.
