Short description

RushTown is a cozy mobile logistics game about running your own company in a river valley: build production chains, run your vans and trucks, and help villages and towns thrive.

Long description

In RushTown you start with one farm and grow a company across a whole valley. Build chains from fields to finished goods, run your own fleet of vans and trucks on real roads, and supply the villages and towns: households move in, homes move up their ladder, and new places open as the story goes on. It plays in short sessions and keeps running while you're away. Ads are optional rewarded videos only.

The icons, portraits, logo and music are made with AI tools, with care.
