About the game

In RushTown you start with one farm and grow a company across a whole valley. Build chains from fields to finished goods, run your own fleet of vans and trucks on real roads, and supply the villages and towns: households move in, homes move up their ladder, and new places open as the story goes on.

It plays in short sessions and keeps running while you're away. Ads are optional rewarded videos only.

Key features

  • Production chains. Grain to flour to bread, sheep to wool to clothes, all the way to steel and solar panels.
  • Your own fleet. Vans and trucks drive real roads; the player plans every route, stop by stop.
  • A living valley. Villages, towns and a harbour city along a river, each with its own homes, needs and people.
  • A story with characters. A dispatcher, an old trucker, a baker, a rival hauler and more, with their own lines.
