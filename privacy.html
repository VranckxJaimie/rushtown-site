RushTown

Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026 · Applies to the RushTown game for Android

The short version

RushTown has no accounts or servers of its own, and your progress is saved on your phone. The game offers optional ads that you choose to watch for a reward. They come from Google AdMob. Once you choose to watch one, Google collects some device data, such as your advertising ID. In the EU and the UK, Google asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. We, the developer, never receive your personal data.

Who we are

RushTown is made by Jaimie Vranckx, an independent developer based in Antwerp, Belgium ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play RushTown.

What we collect

We do not receive any personal data about you or how you play. RushTown has no servers, accounts, analytics or crash reporting of its own. Two features are provided by Google, which collects data under its own privacy policy: the optional ads and the optional Google Play Games sign-in. Both are described below.

Ads

RushTown shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, for example to double your earnings for a while, finish a building at once or get your next daily gift early. You can watch up to 6 a day. There are no banner or pop-up ads, and you never need to watch an ad to progress.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the game may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • how you interact with the ads and the app, such as app starts, ads shown and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data.

Google's ads software only starts the first time you tap a "Watch an ad" button. After that, it may load the next ad in advance so that one is ready. This data goes to Google, not to us, and is encrypted in transit. Google explains how it uses this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

We ask for ads that are suitable for all ages.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads are personalised. You can change it at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & ads choices, which appears once you have watched your first ad. Without consent, Google may show non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, usually under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Not watching. Ads are always optional. If you never tap a "Watch an ad" button, the ads software never starts and Google receives nothing from it.

Google Play Games

You can sign in with your Google Play Games profile from the profile screen. If you are already signed in to Play Games on your phone, the game may recognise you automatically, and Google may show a short "Welcome back" message.

When you are signed in, the game reads your Play Games player name and player ID to show your name on the profile screen. It does not store them or send them to us. Google handles the sign-in under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can manage or delete your Play Games profile at play.google.com/games/profile. Signing in is optional, and nothing in the game requires it.

What stays on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game stores these in its own private storage on your device:

Your saved game
Your towns, buildings, trucks, coins and progress, plus the company name and logo you choose.
Save backups
A copy of an older save, kept when a test version starts a new world.
Your settings
Graphics level, interface size, sound volumes, language, and which version of this policy you have seen.
Your ad choices
The consent choice you made in Google's consent form, stored on your device by Google's consent software.
A player number
A random number shown on your profile card. It is made on your device, is not linked to your identity and is never sent anywhere.
Loading files
Prepared world data that helps the game start faster.
A shared picture
The last picture of your town you shared, kept so your phone's share menu can send it.

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling RushTown, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes all of it.

Sharing pictures

When you tap a Share button, the game takes a picture of your town, without the game's menus, and opens your phone's share menu with a short text and a link to RushTown on Google Play. You choose the app to share it with. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.

Checking for updates

To tell you when a new version is out, or when this policy has changed, the game downloads a small public file from GitHub with the newest version numbers. Nothing about you or your game is sent with this request. As with any download, GitHub sees your device's IP address while it delivers the file, under GitHub's privacy statement; we never see it.

Device permissions

RushTown does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files. It uses the internet for the update check, the ads and Google Play Games sign-in. Google's ads software also uses some standard permissions that need no prompt, such as access to your advertising ID and your network state. You can play without a connection; only ads and sign-in need one.

Links to other sites

The game's profile screen has buttons that open our community pages, such as Discord, and this policy in your web browser. Those sites are run by other companies, and their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Google Play

When you download RushTown from Google Play, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy. RushTown has no in-app purchases yet. If we add them, payments will be handled by Google Play; we would receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, never your payment details. We will update this policy before any such change.

Children

RushTown is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and we ask Google for ads that are suitable for all ages.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to share or remove. For the data Google collects for ads, you can change your consent in the game, reset your advertising ID, or use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. The game also shows a short notice the next time you open it.
