RushTown

Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · Applies to the RushTown game for Android

The short version

RushTown does not collect, sell or share any personal data. The game runs on your device, has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your progress is saved on your phone and nowhere else.

Who we are

RushTown is made by Jaimie Vranckx, an independent developer based in Antwerp, Belgium ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play RushTown.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not receive any information about you or how you play. We do not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking services, and RushTown has no servers of its own.

What stays on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game stores these files in its own private storage on your device:

Your saved game
Your towns, buildings, trucks, coins and progress, plus the company name and logo you choose.
Your settings
Graphics level, interface size and sound volumes.
A player number
A random number shown on your profile card. It is made on your device, is not linked to your identity and is never sent anywhere.
Loading files
Prepared world data that helps the game start faster.

None of this leaves your device. Uninstalling RushTown, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes all of it.

Checking for updates

To tell you when a new version is out, the game downloads a small public file from GitHub that lists the newest version number. Nothing about you or your game is sent with this request. As with any download, GitHub sees your device's IP address while it delivers the file, under GitHub's privacy statement; we never see it.

Device permissions

RushTown does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files. It uses internet access only for the update check above, and it does not need a connection to play.

Links to other sites

The game's profile screen has buttons that open our community pages (such as Discord or Instagram) and this policy in your web browser. Those sites are run by other companies, and their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Google Play

When you download RushTown from Google Play, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy. If we add in-app purchases later, payments will be handled by Google Play; we would receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, never your payment details. We will update this policy before any such change.

Children

RushTown is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal data, it does not collect any from children either.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Since we hold none, there is nothing for us to share or remove, but you are welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
