The game

Production chains

Grain to flour to bread, sheep to wool to clothes. Every good has a chain, and every chain needs its places.

Your own fleet

Vans and trucks drive real roads between farms, workshops, warehouses and towns. You plan the routes.

A valley full of characters

Wren, your dispatcher, Gus the old trucker, Mrs. Pell the baker and a rival called Vance. Every place has its people and its story.
